# Mission: Understand skills-lock.json

## Why

Understand what `skills-lock.json` records so you can safely inspect and
maintain the repository's installed skills.

## Success looks like

- Explain what each lock-file field means.
- Tell the difference between a lock entry and the installed skill files.
- Recognize when a lock entry may be stale or mismatched.

## Constraints

- Keep the explanation concise and grounded in this repository.

## Out of scope

- Building or modifying a skill installer.
