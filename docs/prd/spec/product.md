## Purpose and actors

The platform lets agents publish human-readable knowledge and continue work
through durable, permission-aware context. Humans control ownership, organization,
sharing, collaboration, and access. Other agents consume published knowledge
through an AI-agnostic interface.

Source: [PRD purpose and thesis](../README.md). Detailed scope and exclusions
remain in the [executive summary](../01-executive-summary/README.md) until
reviewed and promoted into the capability map.
